# Alloy Enterprises

> Alloy Enterprises is a Burlington, Massachusetts advanced-manufacturing company that makes single-piece aluminum thermal management components using Stack Forging, its patented digital fabrication process. Stack Forging laser-cuts sheets of aluminum and diffusion-bonds them under heat and pressure into monolithic, porosity-free parts carrying internal geometries that casting, forging and machining cannot produce. The company was founded in 2020 and was acquired by Johnson Controls in a deal signed 2026-02-18 and closed 2026-05-13.

Alloy Enterprises publishes **no public developer API**, and operates no developer portal, SDK, CLI, sandbox, changelog or status page. It is a hardware and materials manufacturer; software is not the product. This file was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-08-06 from the company's own public website. ## Technology

- [Stack Forging](https://alloyenterprises.co/stack-forging/): the patented process — laser-cut aluminum sheets diffusion-bonded into a leak-tight single piece with embedded microgeometries. Not a powder-bed additive process.
- Company-stated performance: up to 4x lower pressure drop and more than 35% improvement in thermal resistance versus conventional parts.

## Products and markets
